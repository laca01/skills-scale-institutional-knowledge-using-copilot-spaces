# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome! This documentation centralizes OctoAcme’s living project management knowledge. Here you’ll find our core workflows, roles, communication patterns, and quality practices—organized for onboarding, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme’s project lifecycle is structured yet adaptable, with stages including Initiation, Planning, Execution & Tracking, Risk Management & Communication, Release & Deployment, and Retrospective & Continuous Improvement.

- **Initiation:** Projects start by validating business need, clarifying success criteria, and aligning stakeholders. Key artifacts (Project One-pager, stakeholder list, timeline) are generated.
- **Planning:** Teams break initiatives into actionable backlogs, estimate effort, set milestones, and identify risks. Acceptance criteria and Definition of Done are used to guide scope and quality.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Delivery is managed on project boards (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, etc.), with daily standups, weekly syncs, demos, and automated/manual testing to ensure standards.
- **Risk Management & Communication:** Risks are logged, assessed, mitigated, and monitored weekly. Status updates and incident templates ensure transparency and escalation paths are defined.
- **Release & Deployment:** Releases follow standardized readiness and rollback plans, smoke testing, stakeholder communication, and post-release monitoring.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement:** After each sprint, teams capture learnings and action items. Improvements are fed back into process docs and tracked via backlogs, supporting continuous refinement.

Roles involved include Project Managers (delivery coordination), Product Managers (outcome definition), Developers, QA/Testers, and Stakeholders. All team insights are versioned and made accessible so new members can onboard quickly and teams can operate consistently.
